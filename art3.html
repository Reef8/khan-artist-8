555: Eye on Ball

Inspired by Jahangir Khan (the G.O.A.T. of squash) and his remarkable 555 match win streak that lasted nearly 5.5 years. This piece was created by hitting paint-covered squash balls onto the canvas 555 times. A unique piece that becomes truly mind-blowing when you consider what each ball mark represents!
